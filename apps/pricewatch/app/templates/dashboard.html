How watches work

Watching — PriceWatch checks retailers on each watch’s schedule.

Strike found — an offer met your target price and conditions. With Notifications ntfy on, you’re alerted when it’s found and again if the price drops further. Re-arm to clear the strike and start fresh.

Retailer scores — ★ scores come from Trustpilot; a muted, outlined badge is PriceRunner’s own shop rating, used only when Trustpilot has none. A watch can require a minimum score: cheaper offers from lower-rated shops are never strikes, but can send a warning.

Error / No results — the last check failed or found no offers; PriceWatch keeps trying on schedule.

Paused — kept with its history, but not checked.

Refresh in the top bar checks every active watch now.
