Add links from anywhere

Save a page to the board without copying and pasting. These use the address you opened Pinboard on: to have them work away from home too, open Pinboard at your Tailscale address before setting them up.

Computer: bookmarklet

Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar. On any page, click it to add that page to the board.

{{ icon("pin") }} Add to Pinboard

iPhone or iPad: Shortcut

Puts "Add to Pinboard" in the share sheet of Safari, YouTube and other apps. One-off set-up in the Shortcuts app:

  1. Tap + to make a new shortcut and name it Add to Pinboard.
  2. Tap the ⓘ (or the settings icon) and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Under share sheet types keep URLs and Safari web pages.
  3. Add the action URL Encode and set it to encode Shortcut Input.
  4. Add the action Text and type this, then insert the URL Encoded Text variable right after it: /add?url=
  5. Add the action Open URLs (it opens the Text). Done: in any app, tap Share, then Add to Pinboard.

Android: share sheet

Open Pinboard in Chrome at its https:// Tailscale address and choose Add to Home screen (or Install app). Pinboard then appears when you tap Share in other apps.
