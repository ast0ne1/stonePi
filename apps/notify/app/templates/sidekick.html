Your voice is turned into text here on the Pi and never leaves it. The text of your question, plus the facts Pebble is allowed to use, go to the AI model above to write the answer.

So you can answer Pebble's follow-up questions ("which category?"), it remembers your last question and its answer for 2 minutes, in memory only; nothing is written down.

Which model answers is set in Dashboard → Settings → AI, under Pebble → Spoken replies. Every Pebble uses the same one.
